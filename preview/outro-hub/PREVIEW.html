Gameplay / balance

What to tune next

Concrete balance notes from the current numbers in js/entities.js / combat loop — not generic advice.

Weapon DPS skew

  • Plasma is currently the runaway pick: 40 dmg / 0.08s ≈ 500 DPS vs gun ≈ 120 DPS and ion ≈ 175 DPS.
  • Suggestion: drop plasma to ~22–26 dmg or slow rate to ~0.14, keep it as a close-range shredder.
  • Rockets (95 + splash 55) are fine as limited burst — maybe start with 6–8 instead of 10 so they stay special.

Difficulty curve

  • Enemy HP only scales +8%/sector; fire rate gets faster — late game feels bullet-hell dense more than tanky.
  • Final boss: 5200 HP, fireRate 0.22 — hard spike vs mid bosses (~1180–1740 HP).
  • Suggestion: add a short calm after sector clear, or give +15 shield on sector enter; soften final phase-1 pattern before escorts.

Systems that fight the fantasy

  • Self-destruct deletes the sky and chips the boss for 260 — strong “panic win.” Cap once/sector or remove boss chunk.
  • Super laser snowballs from kills — great fantasy, but can erase authored tension. Gate charge gain in boss rooms.
  • Polarity (black/white) is cosmetic only — either wire match rules or stop implying Chromium-style color play.
  • Pass-up powerups for score is clever; surface it in HUD (“LET PASS +500”) so players learn it.
