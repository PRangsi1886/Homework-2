Mission Briefing

  • Survive the gauntlet. Destroy hostiles and dodge fire — ships that slip past the bottom simply leave.
  • Ram them. Your hull is a weapon. Collisions hurt both ships.
  • Conserve ammo. Gun is endless. Ion pierces. Plasma hits hard. Rockets (4) home in and blast a radius.
  • Strategic suicide. Press 0 twice to eject ammo and clear the sky for the next fighter.
  • Power-ups. Grab shield / repair / super shield / rockets — or let them pass for score (super shield = extra life).
  • Four sectors. Bosses guard sectors 1–3. Sector 4 ends with a three-phase final commander.
